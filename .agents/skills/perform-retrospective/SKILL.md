# Perform Retrospective

Establish the reviewed period, permitted evidence and project boundaries.
Prefer durable repository evidence and do not assume access to private chat or
sensitive material. Separate project-specific findings from reusable workflow,
source-template and family-wide candidates.

For each candidate record evidence, recurrence, likely destination, benefit,
risk and open decision. Sanitize anything leaving the project boundary. Route
approved proposals to the appropriate template or governance `IDEAS.md`; do not
edit another repository or promote a proposal automatically.
